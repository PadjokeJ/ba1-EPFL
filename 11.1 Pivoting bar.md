A bar that falls from a position, suspended from a string. Behaves closely to a pendulum.

We have the angle of rotation defined by either
$$\begin{array}l
\theta & = \text{angle from }Ox \\
\phi   & = \pi - \theta
\end{array}$$

We have two possibilities, either the object hits the ground and stops oscillating, or continues swinging like a pendulum 

## Case pendulum

We use the formula
$$\ddot \phi + \frac32 \frac gl \sin\phi = 0$$
Using small angle angle approximation for $\phi = \sin \phi$
