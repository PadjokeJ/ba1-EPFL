#aicc 

We have
$$(x+y)^n = \sum_{j = 0}^n \left( \begin{array}c n \\ j \end{array} \right) x^{n-j}y^j$$
>[!Corollary]
>$$\sum_{k=0}^n \left( \begin{array}c n \\ k \end{array} \right) = 2^n$$

>[!Theorem]
>Pascal's Identity
>$$\left( \begin{array}c n + 1 \\ k \end{array} \right) = \left( \begin{array}c n \\ k - 1 \end{array} \right) + \left( \begin{array}c n \\ k \end{array} \right)$$

We can show this as pascal's triangle
$$\begin{array}c 
  \left( \begin{array}c 
    0 \\ 0 
  \end{array} \right) 
\\
  \left( \begin{array}c 
    1 \\ 0 
  \end{array} \right) \quad 
  \left( \begin{array}c 
    1 \\ 1 
  \end{array} 
  \right) 
\\
  \left( \begin{array}c 
    2 \\ 0 
  \end{array} \right) \quad 
  \left( \begin{array}c 
    2 \\ 1 
  \end{array} \right)\quad 
  \left( \begin{array}c 
    2 \\ 2 
  \end{array} \right)
\\
  \left( \begin{array}c 
    3 \\ 0 
  \end{array} \right) \quad 
  \left( \begin{array}c 
    3 \\ 1 
  \end{array} \right) \quad 
  \left( \begin{array}c 
    3 \\ 2 
  \end{array} \right) \quad 
  \left( \begin{array}c 
    3 \\ 3 
  \end{array} \right)
\end{array}$$
