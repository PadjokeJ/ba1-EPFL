A mass $m$ slides without friction on a horizontal axis. It is attached to a spring with a stiffness of $k$ and rest length $l_0$.

We pull it off of its rest position, and release it. 
This is a free harmonic oscillator

The weight's forces cancel out vertically, so we only have to analyse this for the $x$ axis. It has a force
$$\vec F_k = -kx\vec e_x$$

We define the **natural angular frequency** of the system as 
$$\Omega_0 = \sqrt \frac km$$
And thus we get the equation
$$\ddot x(t)+\Omega_0^2x(t)=0$$
The solution of the system can be rewritten as
$$\begin{array}l
x(t) =& A * \cos(\Omega_0t)+ B * \sin(\Omega_0t) \\ 
x(t) =& C * \cos(\Omega_0t + \phi) \\
x(t) =& D * \sin(\Omega_0t + \psi) \end{array}
$$
We then have to find the constants A B C D that solve the equations, first using $A$ and $B$ and the initial conditions of the system.

The motion, which is periodic, has a period of
$$T_0 = \frac{2\pi}{\Omega_0} = 2\pi\sqrt\frac mk$$
