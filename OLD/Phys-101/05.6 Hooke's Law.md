#phys 

The force of a spring that tries to restore its original position when it is stretched or compressed. 
- When no force is applied, it rests at a natural length called the rest length $l_0$
- When the string is deformed, its length is $l = l_0 +x$, where $x$ is the deformation from its original length

In the ideal case, we have Hooke's Law: the force is proportional to the deformation. We simplify the deformation to a single axis $\vec e_x$
$$\vec F_k = -kx\vec e_x$$
- $k$ is the stiffness constant of the spring in $[N/m]$

>[!Note]
>We can rewrite the formula using the definition of the deformation.
>$$\vec F_k = -k(l-l_0)\vec e_x$$
