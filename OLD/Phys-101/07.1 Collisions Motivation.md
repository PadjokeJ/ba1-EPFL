#phys

To determine the motion of an object after a collision, we must analyze the external forces : weight, tension etc... ==But mostly the force exerted by the the other mass that it collides with. ==

However to simplify the problem, we consider the force of collision as an internal force. 
The sum of internal forces will be zero due to the collision being an equal force both ways.
And since the collision is very short, we can ignore the other forces during the short amount of time that the collision takes place in
