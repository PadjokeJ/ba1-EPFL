#phys 

When an object rotates around an axis, we have to introduce the notion of **Moment of inertia** of a solid. It is defined based on an axis around which the object rotates. We have 
$$I_z = \int_{\text{vol}} \rho(\vec r) R^2 dV$$

We can therefore rewrite the kinetic rotational energy as 
$$E_{c, \text{rot}} = \frac12 I_z \; \omega^2$$
