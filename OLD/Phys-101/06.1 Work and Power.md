#phys

>[!TLDR] Definition
>The work $W$ of a sum of forces is the amount of movement this force causes. 
>It is expressed in Joules $[\mathrm J]$, where $1 \;\mathrm J = 1 \;\mathrm {N\cdot m} = 1 \mathrm{\;kg\cdot m^2 \cdot s ^{-2}}$  

>[!TLDR] Definition
>The power $P$ is the variation of the work $W$ overtime. We have $\delta W$ and $dt$ then
>$$P={\delta W \over dt} = \vec F \cdot {\vec{dr}\over dt} = \vec F \cdot \vec v$$
>It is expressed in Watts $[\mathrm W]$ where $1\; \mathrm W = 1\; \mathrm{J\cdot s^{-1}}$
