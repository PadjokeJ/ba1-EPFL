#phys 

**Mass** is the representation of the amount of matter. It is an *extensive quantity*, meaning that a system where we combine two masses, then the sum of the masses is the total ammount $m_1 + m_2$. In classical mechanics, mass is conserved

**Momentum** is a vector of *extensive quantity*, used to represent the state of motion of an object. Its formula is $$\vec p = m \cdot \vec v$$
A system with two objects having two different masses $m_1$ and $m_2$ and two velocities $v_1$ and $v_2$, will have its momentum characterized as $$\vec p = \vec p_1 + \vec p_2$$
Like mass, momentum is a conserved quantity.
