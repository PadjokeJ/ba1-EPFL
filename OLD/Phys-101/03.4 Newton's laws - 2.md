The change of motion of an object is proportional to the intensity of the force that is causing it. The change of motion happens in the exact same direction of that force. $$\Delta \vec p = \vec F \Delta t$$
We can express this as $$d\vec p = \vec F dt$$
Where $\vec F$ is the sum of all the forces acting on the object. We can rewrite it therefore as $$\sum \vec F = \frac{d\vec p}{dt} \iff \frac{d(m \vec v)}{dt} \iff m \frac{d\vec v}{dt} \iff m \vec a$$
We therefore get that the sum of all the forces on an object, is its mass times the acceleration vector
