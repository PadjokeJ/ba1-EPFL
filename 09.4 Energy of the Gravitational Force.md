Potential Energy
---
We seek to find the work done between two points by the gravitational force:
$$W_{AB}^{\vec F_G} = \int_A^B \left( - {GMm \over r²} \right) \vec e_r \cdot \vec d_r$$
we can write $\vec{d r}$ as a function of the velocity in spherical coordinates
$$\vec{d r} = \vec v dt = (\dot r \vec e_r + r \dot\theta \vec e_\theta + r \dot\phi \sin\theta \vec e_\phi) dt$$
-> We then insert it into $W_{AB}^{\vec F_G}$
$$W_{AB}^{\vec F_G} = GMm \int_A^B \left( - \frac1{r^2} \right) \dot r dt$$
==We can then deduce that the work is given by==  write on #cheatsheet
$$ E_P^G = - {GMm \over r}$$
