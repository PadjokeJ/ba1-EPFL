## Position

We can talk about a trajectory using these specific denominations:
$\overrightarrow r (t_x) = \overrightarrow{OP_x}$ , the position $P$ of the object at the given time $t_x$ 

A movement would be $\Delta \overrightarrow r$

## Instant velocity

Between two positions taken at certain time frames, we get the displacement vector $\Delta \overrightarrow r$. By decreasing the time interval $\Delta t$ until it is infinitely small, we get its instant velocity $\overrightarrow v (t) = \lim_{dt  \to 0} \frac{d\overrightarrow r}{dt}$.
the instantanious velocity vector is tangent to the trajectory vector.
To simplify the derivative, we can write $\overrightarrow v = \dot{\overrightarrow r}$ .

## Instant acceleration

Represents the change of velocity $\Delta \overrightarrow v$ 
Same way as we can simplify [[#Instant velocity]] notation, we can write $\overrightarrow a = \ddot{\overrightarrow r}$
