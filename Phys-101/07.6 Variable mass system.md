Let's do this for a rocket. 

We cannot simply apply Netwon's second law $\vec F = m\vec a$, because the mass is no longer constant.

We have a rocket at $t$ with mass $m(t)$ and velocity $v(t)$
- initial mass $m_0$
- The gases are ejected at a constant rate and velocity
- After a time $t + dt$, the rocket has ejected a mass of $-dm$
