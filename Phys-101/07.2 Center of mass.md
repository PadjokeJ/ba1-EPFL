The center of mass $G$ of the system is defined as the average of position and masses. 
$$\overrightarrow {OG} = {m_1\vec r_1 + \dots + m_N\vec r_N \over m_1 + \dots + m_N}$$
>[!Warning] Despite its name, it's a position of length units

It can be used to define a reference frame, where $\vec v_G$ can be used to express its movement. If all the external forces sum up to $0$, then the reference frame based on the center of mass is a Galilean reference frame.
