>[!Reminder]
>-> The planets revolve around the sun in elliptical orbits, with the sun being in one of the foci (focus point of the ellipse)
>-> The areas swept by the radius vectors are proportional to the times taken to sweep them
>-> The squares of the orbital periods of the planets, are proportional to the cubes of the major axes of their orbit

>[!Theorem] 
>The general Gravitational Force is defined as
>$$\vec F_G = - {GMm \over r^2}\vec e_r$$
>with the gravitational constant $G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11}[m^3g^{-1}s^{-2}]$

# Simple case -- Circular motion

We have the expression of velocity of a planet given by 
$$v = \sqrt{GM \over R}$$
The period--which is the time required to complete a revolution around the sun--is given by
$$T = {2\pi R \over v} = 2\pi \sqrt {R^3\over GM}$$
We can also calculate the mass of the sun
$$M = {4\pi^2R^3 \over GT^2}$$
