Distance traveled in a given trajectory

[[01.1-2 Position velocity acceleration and trajectory|Speed]] is given by the formula $v(t) = ds / dt$ 

To represent the coordinates, we can imagine a circle that is colinear at a given point of the trajectory.

$\overrightarrow \tau$ is tangent to the circle, in the direction of the motion  
$\overrightarrow n$ points to the center of the circle (perpendicular to the circle)  
$\rho$ is the radius of the circle

therefore $\overrightarrow v = v * \overrightarrow \tau = \frac{ds}{dt} \overrightarrow \tau$ 
and $\overrightarrow a (t) = \frac{d}{dt} (v \overrightarrow \tau)$ $= \frac{dv}{dt} \overrightarrow \tau + \frac{v ^2}{\rho}\overrightarrow n$
