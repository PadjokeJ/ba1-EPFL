We have a cylinder rolling down a slope. Here we have to apply the angular momentum theorem to solve the problem at a fixed point.

We have the center of mass $G$, the origin $O$, and the point of contact $A$

We have the general formula for an arbitrary point $A$ given by 
$${d \vec L_A \over dt} = \sum \vec M_A - M_{\vec v_{\mathcal R}}(A) \ \wedge \ \vec v_{\mathcal R} (G)$$
But since the velocity of $A$ is colinear to the one of $G$, then we can eliminate the second term of the formula, and get
$${d \vec L_A \over dt} = \sum \vec M_A$$
