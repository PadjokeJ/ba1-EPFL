$|e_x| = |e_y| = |e_z| = 1$ 

we can write $\overrightarrow r = \overrightarrow{OP} | x y z$ and $\overrightarrow r = x \overrightarrow e_x + y \overrightarrow e_y + z \overrightarrow e_z$ 

The cartesian coordinates are fixed, therefore their derivative is always 0. 

>[!Warning]
>But the object position can change over time, therefore x, y and z can change over time.
>$x = x(t)$ is implicit and never mentionned

$\overrightarrow a = \dot v_x \overrightarrow e_x + \dot v_y \overrightarrow e_y + \dot v_z \overrightarrow e_z$
$\overrightarrow a = \ddot x \overrightarrow e_x + \ddot y \overrightarrow e_y + \ddot z \overrightarrow e_z$
