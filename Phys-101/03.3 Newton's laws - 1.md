## So what is a force?

A force is an action made by one object unto another. They can come in all sorts of types :
- Fundamental Forces (natural)
- Phenomenological Forces (a consequence of fundamental forces)
- Inertial/Fictitious Forces, WHICH ARE NOT DUE TO THE ACTIONS OF AN OBJECT ONTO ANOTHER

There can be made a difference of the origin of a force. We can say that a force is either INTERNAL or EXTERNAL
