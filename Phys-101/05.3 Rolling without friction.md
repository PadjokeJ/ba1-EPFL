In this section we will prove that when a wheel rolls without slipping, then the velocity between the point of contact and the ground is $0$

We have a wheel of radius $R$, center $A$ in the referential $\mathcal R (x,y)$ and $C$ the point of contact of the ground

We attach a reference frame $\mathcal R' (A, x',y')$, which is moving in $\mathcal R$ at the velocity $\vec v_t$.
By definition this means that we have 
$$\vec v_{\mathcal R}(C)=\vec 0$$
$$\vec v_{\mathcal R}(C) = \vec v_{\mathcal R}(A) + \vec v_{\mathcal R'}(C)\quad\implies\quad \vec v_{\mathcal R'}(C)=-\vec v_t$$
The wheel rotates at a constant velocity of $\omega$, therefore the velocity of $C$ in $\mathcal R'$ is 
$$v_t = R\omega$$
For a point $B$ diametrically opposed to $C$, we find
$$\vec v_{\mathcal R'}(B)=\vec v_t$$
$$\vec v_{\mathcal R}(B) = 2\vec v_t$$
