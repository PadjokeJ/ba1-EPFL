Momentum is conserved, but not kinetic energy which is dissipated as heat. 

We still find the equations
$$\begin{array}l
\vec P_\text{tot} &=& m_1\vec v_1 + m_2\vec v_2 \\
\vec P^{'}_\text{tot} &=& (m_1+m_2)\vec v \\
\vec P_\text{tot} &=& \vec P^{'}_\text{tot}
\end{array}$$
By isolatinv $\vec v$, we find
$$\vec v = {m_1\vec v_1 + m_2 \vec v_2 \over m_1 + m_2}$$
