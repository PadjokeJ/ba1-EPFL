These are principles of physics which allows us to connect the forces to motions, and allow us to predict, analyse and understand them. 

They don't need to be proved, and are given.

Newton came up with them when he realised that it's not the speed of gravity that is constant, but its acceleration. This was unlike the line of thought of the time.

He deduced that $$\begin{align} \text{if } & \vec{v} = \vec{cste} & \text{ then } & \vec{F} = \vec{0} \end{align}$$
