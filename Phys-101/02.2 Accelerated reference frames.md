We have a point $P$ that moves in our referential $\mathcal R'$ $(A, y_1, y_2, y_3)$ $\mathcal R'$ moves in $\mathcal R$


The vector OP can be decomposed into the vectors OA + AP
So the position of P is *the position of A* + *the position of P in $\mathcal R'$*

To get the velocity vector, we NEED the derivative of OP

$\frac{\delta}{\delta t} (\vec{OP}) = \frac{\delta}{\delta t} (\vec{OA} + \vec{AP})$
$\vec v_{\mathcal R} (P) = \frac{\delta}{\delta t} (OA) + \frac{\delta}{\delta t} (AP)$
$\vec v_{\mathcal R} (P) = \vec v_{\mathcal R}(A) + \sum_i\frac{\delta}{\delta t}(y_i \vec e_{y_i})$ 
$\vec v_{\mathcal R} (P) = \vec v_{\mathcal R}(A) +\vec v_{\mathcal R'}(P) + \vec \omega \wedge \vec{AP}$
