#phys 
As we have seen with the 3 [[03.1 Newton's laws|laws of newton]], for an inertial reference frame, the sum of all forces is equal to the object's acceleration scaled by its mass. ([[03.4 Newton's laws - 2|Newton's second law]])
We also need a new unit to better analyse the forces. We call those "Newtons", and are defined as $$1N = 1 \frac{{kg} \cdot m}{s^2}$$
To analyse a force, we can use the following steps:
1. Identify the system
2. Choose the right reference frame
3. Choose a type of right-handed or orthonormal [[01.3  Coordinate systems|coordinate system]]
4. Draw a diagram representing the situation
5. Draw the forces
6. List the conditions of the system
7. Solve the equations
	1. As generalities, restrained to the conditions
	2. Then with the given units and values (if they exist)
