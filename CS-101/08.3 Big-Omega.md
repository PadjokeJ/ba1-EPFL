We say that $f(x)\in \Omega(g(x))$ if there are two constants $C$ and $k$, such as 
$$\vert f(x)\vert \ge C\vert g(x)\vert,\quad x\gt k$$
We say f is big-Omega of g
big-Omega tells us that a function grows at least as fast as another (lower bound)

$f(x)$ is $\Omega (g(x))$ <-> $g(x)$ is $O(g(x))$
