#aicc 
This course is about discrete maths: study of structures that have a finite amount of objects.
## Why ?

- Structure of PCs is discrete  
- Data processing is discrete  
- Structure and the logic of programs is discrete  

Used for checking if algorithms are optimised, working and to make them.  
## What

We will look at problems with "pen and paper": No programming language. The other cs course is for coding.

we need:
- Common sense
- Logic and math reasoning
- Basic structures
- Algorithms 
- Counting
- Probability theory (what to expect from the algorithm in an average run)

Based on the book *Discrete Mathematics and its Applications* by Kennedy Rosen
