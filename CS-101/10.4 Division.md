#aicc 

If $a$ and $b$ are two integers, with $a\neq 0$, we have the result of the division $c$ given by $b=ac$
We write it
$$c={b\over a}$$

We write $a \mid b$ if $a$ divides $b$, else we write $a \cancel\mid b$ 

>[!theorem]
>If $a$ is an integer and $d$ an integer, with $d\gt0$, then we have unique integers $q$ and $r$, with $0\le r\lt d$,
>$$a = dq + r$$
>- $d$ is the divisor
>- $a$ is the dividend
>- $q$ is the quotient
>- $r$ is the remainder

We write
$$q = a \mathrm{ \;div\; } d$$
$$r = a \mod d$$
