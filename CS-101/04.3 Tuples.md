# Cartesian Product

Tuples are used for the cartesian product, as the result of the product of sets

>[!Example]-
>For two sets $A$ and $B$
>$$A \times B = \{(a,b) \mid a \in A \land b \in B\}$$

>[!Warning]
>The cartesian product is not commutative nor associative, as tuples are **ordered**
