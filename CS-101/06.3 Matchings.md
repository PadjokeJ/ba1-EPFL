#aicc 

With a set $A$, a matching of $A$ is a set of unordered pairs of distinct elements of $A$, of which each element only appears in maximum one pair.

A **maximum** matching is a matching with the largest amount of independant pairs.
# Preferences

A preference list $L_x$ defines for every element $x\in A$, the order of preference that the element $x$ would be matched with another.

# Stability

A matching is unstable if there are two pairs don't have their mutual best preference.

## Marriage Problem

Find a maximum stable matching for two disjointed subsets of a subset

### Gale-Shapley alg

1. Proposal
	Men propose to women in their order of preference
2. Acceptance/Rejection
	If a woman is free, she accepts
	If she is engaged she compares
	- If she prefers the new one, she dumps the old and accepts the new
	- else she rejects
3. Continue
	Any man who is rejected proposes to the next woman on his list
4. Termination
	The process continues until every man is matched

This guarantees a stable matching
