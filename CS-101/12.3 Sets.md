## Principle of Inclusion-Exclusion

We have seen that
$$\vert A \cup B \vert = \vert A \vert + \vert B \vert - \vert A \cap B \vert$$
This principle also applies to 3 sets, 
$$\vert A \cup B \cup C \vert = \vert A \vert + \vert B \vert + \vert C \vert - \vert A \cap B \vert - \vert A \cap B \vert - \vert A \cap C \vert - \vert B \cap C \vert$$

>[!Theorem]
>Include the cardinalities of single sets
>Exclude pairwise intersections
>Exclude triplewise intersections
>...
>Exclude until the cardinality of the $n$-tuple-wise intersection is included ($n$ odd), excluded $n$ even
>$$\vert A_1 \cup A_2 \cup \dots \cup A_n \vert = \sum_{i = 1}^n \vert A_i \vert - \sum_{1 \le i \lt k \le n} \vert A_i \cap A_k \vert + \dots + (-1)^{n+1} \left\vert \bigcap_{1 \le i \le n} A_i \right\vert$$
