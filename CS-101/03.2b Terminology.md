## Definitions

A way to establish something, you can base it on axioms

>[!Example]
>An integer $n$ is even if there exists an integer $k$, such that $n = 2k$

## Theorem

>[!Example]
>Theorem: $(a + b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2$
## Axioms

A statement which is given as true, without any proof
You can not reduce a axiom to any lower form

>[!Example]-
>Let's prove $1+1=2$
>We have the axioms:
>1. Zero is a natural number $0 \in \mathbb N$
>2. Every natural number has a successor $\forall n \in \mathbb N, S(n) \in \mathbb N$
>We know $1:= S(0)$
>$2:= S(1) = S(S(0))$
>Therefore :
>$1 + 1 = 1 + S(0) = S(0 + 1) = S(1) = 2$
