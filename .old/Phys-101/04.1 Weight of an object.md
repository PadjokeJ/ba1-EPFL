In ballistics, since we assume an object is only affected by the force of gravity, we can therefore say that its motion is only affected by its weight. 
We usually use as a referential our lab, and therefore suppose that the earth is flat and the acceleration is constant and directed downwards.
We call the force acting on our object $\vec P$, which is given by$$\vec P = m \vec g$$
with $g = ||\vec g|| = 9.8 m/s^2$ 

>[!Warning]
>The mass of an object is a property that usually doesn't change. IT IS NOT THE SAME THIS AS THE WEIGHT OF AN OBJECT. The weight of an object changes depending on the scale of gravity of the given referential

We can approximate rather well that the force of gravity points to the center of the Earth.
