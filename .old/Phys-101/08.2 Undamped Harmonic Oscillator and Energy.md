The mechanical energy of the system can be represented as
$$E_m = E^k_p + E_m = \frac12kx^2+\frac12mv^2$$
After simplification we find that
$$E_m = \frac12kx_0^2 = \text{constant}$$

>[!Warning] For the oscillator to be harmonic, it is necessary and sufficient that the potential energy takes the following form:
>$$E_p = A(x-x_0)^2+E_{p,0},\qquad A\gt0$$

If we define $X = x-x_0$, we get (and remember, $A\gt0$)
$$\ddot X+\Omega_0^2X=0$$
