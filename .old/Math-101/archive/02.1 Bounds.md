### Theorem
$x = \text{sup}(s)$ $\iff$ $\forall s \in S$, $x \ge s$ and $\forall \epsilon \gt 0$ $\implies$ $s \ge x - \epsilon$ 

x is the supremum of S ==if and only if== for all s in the set S, x is greater or equal to s and all epsilon are greater than zero, such that s is greater or equal than x minus epsilon
